Beads wavetables

12 banks of 16 waves; FEEDBACK picks the bank in the Beads synth. Draw on the large wave, or import. Keys: left and right pick 16 samples (Shift: one), up and down move them.

Wave terrain grids

Up to 16 terrains of 64 × 64 for Plaits' Wave terrain. Use x and y (−1 to 1), r, theta, mu, pi, + − * / %, sin cos tan atan atan2 tanh sqrt exp log pow(a, b) abs sign floor ceil round min max and ball(x, y, size).

Import, drawing and preview follow Mutable Instruments' Plaits editor (MIT). The factory waves are Mutable Instruments' Beads tables (MIT).
