How to install

  1. Disconnect your speakers (the update is loud) and all CV cables. Close other apps that make sound.
  2. Patch your audio output to IN L. Set TIME about halfway.
  3. Power on holding FREEZE, then play the update. Yellow on [A] means the level is right; adjust with TIME.

All LEDs blinking red means the level was wrong: press FREEZE and play it again louder. Other problems: see the Beads manual.

Stuck at startup? Read the storage recovery warnings: recovery erases settings and may reset calibration.
